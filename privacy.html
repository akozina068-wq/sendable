Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

What we collect

Your CV and the job advert. When you press “Write my application”, the text you paste is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce your cover letter and answers. We don’t ask for your name, and we don’t store what you paste on our servers. Please don’t paste anything you wouldn’t want processed this way.

Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email and the amount paid. We never see your card details.

Why we collect it

To write your application text, give paying users access, send receipts, and handle refunds. We don’t sell your data or share it with other companies for their marketing.

Where it’s stored

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Payment records are kept by Stripe for as long as the law requires.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel to measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to visits and sign-ups. It only loads if you click “OK” on our cookie banner; click “No thanks” and it never loads. When it does load, it may set cookies and send Meta information about your visit, such as the page viewed and whether you subscribed. You can control ad tracking in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings and in your browser.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Reply to any email we send you, or message us on Instagram @adhd.homecoming, and we’ll act on it within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

About us

Sendable is an adhd.homecoming project. It prepares application text from information you provide. You review and submit every application yourself. Not a recruitment agency; no job outcome is guaranteed.

Refunds

Sendable is £6/week and you can cancel any time in one tap. If it isn’t useful, reply to your Stripe receipt within 7 days and we’ll refund you in full.
